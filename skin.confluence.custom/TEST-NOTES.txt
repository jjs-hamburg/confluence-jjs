5.0.83 synchronized lyrics scrolling / Enter toggle

Base: 5.0.82 + CU LRC 6.7.1.1 JJS.

Test:
1. Start a track with synchronized LRC lyrics and open the Lyrics page.
2. The text should follow playback automatically; the current line should remain around visible line 5 when possible.
3. Press Enter/OK on the Lyrics page: synchronized scrolling stops and the current scroll position remains unchanged.
4. Scroll manually with Up/Down: the service must not reset the position.
5. Press Enter/OK again: the text immediately jumps to the current playback position and resumes synchronized scrolling.
6. The next track starts with synchronized scrolling enabled again.
7. Plain-text lyrics without LRC timestamps: Enter/OK has no effect; manual scrolling remains unchanged.
8. Left/Right navigation, credits, song list, automatic popup and Stop behavior must remain unchanged.
